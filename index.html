Questions Engineers Ask

Which RA8P1 parts are dual-core?

Dual-core parts add a 250 MHz Cortex-M33 next to the Cortex-M85. The letter after R7KA8P1 tells you: J and K are dual-core, A and B are single-core. B and K also have MIPI CSI-2 and DSI.3

What is the difference between the Ta and Tj grades?

Parts rated −40 to 105 °C (Ta) run the Cortex-M85 at up to 800 MHz and the Cortex-M33 at 200 MHz. Parts rated 0 to 95 °C (Tj) run at up to 1 GHz and 250 MHz. In the part number, the third letter after R7KA8P1 is D for the Ta grade and L for the Tj grade.1,3

Does the EK-RA8P1 include a camera and display?

Yes. It ships with a MIPI graphics expansion board (7-inch, 1024 × 600 capacitive touch display) and a camera expansion board with an Arducam OV5640 5 MP module.4

Where do I get CAD models for Altium Designer?
